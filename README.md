# CompTIA A+ Core 1 and Core 2 Study Notes

A free, complete study guide for CompTIA A+ V15: **Core 1 (220-1201)** and **Core 2 (220-1202)**. For each of the nine official domains you get a plain-English explanation of every objective, the tables you need cold (ports, Wi-Fi standards, cables and connectors, RAM and storage, Windows tools and commands, malware removal steps), how the questions look, a method, the traps that cost points, and original practice items with full explanations: 16 across the domains, plus a 9-item diagnostic. Written for people heading into their first help desk or IT support job. Every exam fact comes from CompTIA's own pages and the official objectives documents, each one [cited](docs/sources.md).

Want to see where you stand first? Take the [free timed CompTIA A+ practice test on PrepClubs](https://prepclubs.com/free-practice-tests/a-plus?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=vv7axy&utm_content=a-plus), or the in-repo [diagnostic](docs/diagnostic.md).

## Start here

1. **Understand the two exams.** Read [how CompTIA A+ works](docs/a-plus-overview.md): you need both cores, in any order, from the same version.
2. **Find your weak domains.** Take the [diagnostic](docs/diagnostic.md) for the core you are sitting next: 5 items for Core 1, 4 for Core 2.
3. **Fix the weakest heavy domain first,** then follow the [7-day plan](docs/study-plans.md#7-day-plan) for that core, or the [2-day plan](docs/study-plans.md#2-day-plan) if your exam is close.

## Quick facts

| | Core 1 | Core 2 |
|---|---|---|
| Exam code | 220-1201 | 220-1202 |
| Covers | Mobile devices, networking, hardware, virtualization and cloud, hardware and network troubleshooting | Operating systems, security, software troubleshooting, operational procedures |
| Questions | A maximum of 90 | A maximum of 90 |
| Question types | Multiple-choice (single and multiple response), drag-and-drop, performance-based | Same |
| Time | 90 minutes | 90 minutes |
| Passing score | 675 (scale of 100 to 900) | 700 (scale of 100 to 900) |
| Launched | March 25, 2025 | March 25, 2025 |
| Languages | English, German, Japanese | English, German, Japanese |

Both: you must pass both exams to earn A+, in any order, and "Core 1 and Core 2 exams must be taken from the same version, no mixing allowed." Recommended experience is 12 months hands-on in an IT support role. Delivered by Pearson VUE at a test center or online. Renewal is on a three-year cycle with 20 continuing education units. Sources: [CompTIA A+](https://www.comptia.org/en-us/certifications/a/), [Core 1 V15](https://www.comptia.org/en-us/certifications/a/core-1-v15/), [Core 2 V15](https://www.comptia.org/en-us/certifications/a/core-2-v15/), [Core 1 and 2](https://www.comptia.org/en-us/certifications/a/core-1-and-2-v15/) and the official objectives documents, checked 24 November 2026.

## How to use this repo

- **Read on GitHub.** Every page has links to the previous page, the next page and back to these contents, so you can read it start to finish like a book.
- **One core at a time.** Most people study and sit one core, then the other. Each core has its own overview page and its own domains.
- **Test yourself honestly.** Answers are hidden under "Answer and explanation". Commit to an answer before you open it.
- **Read every explanation,** including for items you got right. Each one says why every wrong option is wrong.
- **Get hands-on.** Open the tools and run the commands the pages describe on a real Windows, macOS or Linux machine. The performance-based questions reward people who have actually clicked through these screens.
- **Practice timed.** A timed run, such as the [free one on PrepClubs](https://prepclubs.com/free-practice-tests/a-plus?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=vv7axy&utm_content=a-plus), teaches the pace.
- **Clone or fork it** to annotate your own copy. The structure also works as a GitBook space: see [SUMMARY.md](SUMMARY.md).
