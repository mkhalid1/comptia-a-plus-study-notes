# Sources

Every exam fact in these notes comes from the sources below, all checked 24 November 2026. If CompTIA changes a page after that date, CompTIA's page wins.

## CompTIA exam pages

- [CompTIA A+ overview](https://www.comptia.org/en-us/certifications/a/): the two-exam structure, target job roles and DoD 8140 work roles.
- [CompTIA A+ Core 1 V15 (220-1201)](https://www.comptia.org/en-us/certifications/a/core-1-v15/): launch date, retirement estimate, maximum questions, question types (multiple-choice single and multiple response, drag-and-drop, performance-based), length, passing score 675, recommended experience, languages, domain weights.
- [CompTIA A+ Core 2 V15 (220-1202)](https://www.comptia.org/en-us/certifications/a/core-2-v15/): the same details for Core 2, including passing score 700 and domain weights.
- [CompTIA A+ Core 1 and 2 V15](https://www.comptia.org/en-us/certifications/a/core-1-and-2-v15/): both exams required, any order, and "Core 1 and Core 2 exams must be taken from the same version, no mixing allowed."
- [CompTIA Network+](https://www.comptia.org/en-us/certifications/network/): recommended experience for Network+ (used in the FAQ).
- [CompTIA Security+ V8](https://www.comptia.org/en-us/certifications/security/v8/): launch date of Security+ V8 (used in the FAQ).

## Objectives documents

- [CompTIA A+ Core 1 (220-1201) V15 exam objectives, document version 4.0](https://lecbyo.files.cmp.optimizely.com/download/34be017cb73211ef8985a6f347fbf652): every Core 1 domain, objective and example list, the test details table, the note that example lists are not exhaustive, the troubleshooting methodology and the statement that it is not tested, the acronym list, and the authorized materials (brain dump) policy.
- [CompTIA A+ Core 2 (220-1202) V15 exam objectives, document version 4.0](https://lecbyo.files.cmp.optimizely.com/download/cefedfb2b8a511ef809306d06d323538): every Core 2 domain, objective and example list, including the malware removal procedure in objective 2.6, the acronym list and the authorized materials policy.

## CompTIA policies and help pages

- [How Are CompTIA Exams Scored?](https://help.comptia.org/hc/en-us/articles/11186025660308-How-Are-CompTIA-Exams-Scored): scaled scores, what CompTIA does not disclose, and what the score report contains.
- [CompTIA Certification Retake Policy](https://www.comptia.org/en-us/resources/test-policies/comptia-certification-retake-policy/): waiting periods, paying for each attempt, retaking a passed exam.
- [Candidate testing policies](https://www.comptia.org/en/resources/test-policies/candidate-testing-policies/): test center and online delivery, online testing rules (recording, no scheduled breaks, rescheduling until start time, no shared devices) and score reports.
- [Earn continuing education units](https://www.comptia.org/en-us/resources/ce/learn/earn-continuing-education-units-ceus/): three-year renewal cycle and CEUs required (A+ 20).

## Search data

- The FAQ questions come from Google "People also ask" results for CompTIA A+ searches, including "comptia a+ 220-1201" (United States, English), collected through DataForSEO on 24 November 2026.

## What is our own guidance

The following are our advice, not CompTIA statements: the estimated number of questions per domain (weight times 90, rounded down), which core to take first, the pacing plan, the methods and traps on each domain page, the study plans and longer timeline, practice score targets, study-time estimates, and answers framed as our view in the FAQ, such as difficulty and whether A+ is worth it.
