# FAQ

Real questions people search for about CompTIA A+, answered briefly. Facts come from CompTIA's own pages, listed in [Sources](sources.md). Where an answer is our opinion or study advice, it says so.

## Do I have to pass both Core 1 and Core 2?

Yes. A+ is earned by passing both Core 1 (220-1201) and Core 2 (220-1202). You can take them in any order, but CompTIA says "Core 1 and Core 2 exams must be taken from the same version, no mixing allowed." See [How CompTIA A+ works](a-plus-overview.md).

## What is the passing score?

Core 1 is 675 and Core 2 is 700, both on a scale of 100 to 900. These are scaled scores, so they do not convert to a fixed percentage of questions. See [scoring](scoring.md).

## Is CompTIA A+ a hard exam?

CompTIA publishes no pass rates, so nobody can give you an official difficulty figure. In our view, the hard part is breadth rather than depth: two exams, nine domains, and many lists to memorize, such as ports, connectors, RAID levels, Windows tools and commands. Each question is usually short, but the scenario questions ask for the BEST or FIRST action, where two options can look right. The [diagnostic](diagnostic.md) will show you quickly where you stand.

## What's harder, A+ Core 1 or 2?

There is no official answer, because CompTIA publishes no pass rates for either. In our view, it depends on your background: people who build PCs or work with networks tend to find Core 1 easier, and people who use Windows tools and the command line every day tend to find Core 2 easier. Core 2 has the higher passing score (700 against 675). If you are unsure which to take first, see [which core to take first](a-plus-overview.md#which-core-to-take-first). Read both overviews, [Core 1](core-1/README.md) and [Core 2](core-2/README.md), and mark which objectives are new to you.

## What is the difference between 220-1101 and 220-1201?

220-1201 is Core 1 of the current A+ version, V15, launched on March 25, 2025. 220-1101 is Core 1 of an earlier A+ version, with its own objectives document. You cannot pair one version's Core 1 with another version's Core 2. These notes cover only V15 (220-1201 and 220-1202); check CompTIA's A+ page for which versions you can currently book.

## Can I pass CompTIA A+ in 2 months?

It depends on your starting point. As our guidance: someone with some IT support or PC-building experience can often be ready for both exams in about two months of steady daily study, roughly a month per core. Someone completely new to IT should plan for three to four months. See the [longer timeline](study-plans.md#a-longer-timeline-for-the-whole-certification).

## How many hours a day should I study for CompTIA A+?

There is no official figure. As our guidance, one to two focused hours a day, most days of the week, works better than long weekend sessions, because so much of A+ is memorization that sticks through repetition. In the final week before each exam, 1.5 to 3 hours a day suits the [7-day plan](study-plans.md#7-day-plan).

## What is the best way to learn CompTIA A+?

In our view: learn from the official objectives, practice hands-on, and test yourself often. Work through the domain pages in order of weight, open every Windows tool and run every command the objectives list, and handle real hardware if you can. Do the practice items on each domain page, review every miss, and finish each core with timed runs. See [study plans](study-plans.md).

## Is CompTIA A+ still worth it?

For people aiming at IT support, help desk and desktop support roles, A+ is one of the most commonly requested entry-level certifications. CompTIA names IT support specialist and help desk technician as target roles, and lists DoD 8140 work roles such as technical support specialist and system administrator. Whether it is worth it for you depends on the jobs you are targeting: read a dozen postings for the role you want and see how often it appears.

## Is CompTIA still worth it in 2026?

CompTIA's current versions are recent: A+ V15 launched in March 2025, and Security+ V8 launched in November 2026. In our view, that is a sign the certifications are still kept current with the job. Their value to you depends on the roles you want, and they work best alongside hands-on experience: a home lab, help desk work, and the ability to explain what you did.

## What is CompTIA A+ salary?

There is no single A+ salary. Pay depends on the role, your experience and your location, and a certification is only one part of what employers pay for. Look at local job postings and salary data for the specific role you want, such as help desk technician or IT support specialist.

## Can I skip A+ and do Network+?

Yes. CompTIA does not require A+ before Network+. For Network+ it recommends A+ plus 9 to 12 months of hands-on experience in a junior network administrator or network support technician role. If you already know PC hardware, Windows and basic troubleshooting, going straight to Network+ can make sense; if you are new to IT, A+ covers the groundwork Network+ assumes.

## How much does the A+ exam cost?

You pay for Core 1 and Core 2 separately. CompTIA lists the current exam price on its site and in its store, and it can vary by country, so check there before you book. Each attempt is paid in full; there are no free retakes.

## Can I take A+ online?

Yes. You can test at a Pearson VUE test center or online through OnVUE. Online exams are recorded through your webcam and have no scheduled breaks. See the [exam-day checklist](exam-day-checklist.md).

## How long is A+ valid?

A+ is renewed on a three-year cycle through CompTIA's continuing education program, which requires 20 continuing education units (CEUs) for A+.

## What happens if I fail?

There is no waiting period between your first and second attempt of an exam; before a third or later attempt, you must wait at least 14 calendar days. Your score report lists the objectives linked to questions you got wrong, which is the best place to start. See [scoring](scoring.md#if-you-do-not-pass).

## Where can I practice for free?

Start with the [diagnostic](diagnostic.md) and the practice items on each domain page in this repo. For a timed run in the real format, take the [free CompTIA A+ practice test on PrepClubs](https://prepclubs.com/free-practice-tests/a-plus?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=vv7axy&utm_content=a-plus).
